# v3.11.0

The scale now weighs Bambu Lab and Creality spools by their own chip, once you
have linked that chip to a spool in Tiger Studio.

## Added

- **Linked chips.** A Bambu Lab or Creality CFS spool carries a MIFARE Classic
  chip the scale cannot read as a TigerTag. Link it to a spool of your inventory
  in Tiger Studio, put the spool on the scale, and the scale weighs that spool:
  brand, material and colour come from your inventory, and the weight is written
  to it (and to its twin when it has one). Both chips of a Bambu spool point to
  the same spool, so it is weighed once. Nothing is written into the chip. A link
  made in Studio is picked up within a minute, without a reboot.

## Fixed

- **Ids such as `TigerData_…` keep their case** instead of being uppercased, and
  their cached brand and material are stored under a key the scale's memory
  accepts.

## Removed

- **Forty translation keys no screen used any more**, in all eleven languages.
  Nothing changes on screen.

## Known issues

- The scale reads a maker's chip only when it sits in front of one of the two
  antennas. If nothing happens, turn the spool a quarter turn.
- The first time a maker's chip is placed, the scale may pause for up
  to four seconds while it asks your account.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update**, or over USB with
`bash scripts/flash.sh --fs`. Both keep your WiFi credentials, TigerTag session
and calibration.
