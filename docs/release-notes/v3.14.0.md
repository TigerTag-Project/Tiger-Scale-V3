# v3.14.0

Your scale is ready to weigh in about three seconds after you switch it on,
instead of twenty. It no longer waits for the WiFi before letting you weigh,
and it finds its way back onto the network on its own if the connection drops.
The on-screen keyboard now matches your language.

## Added

- **The on-screen keyboard follows your language.** French uses AZERTY,
  German uses QWERTZ, and every other language keeps QWERTY. The letters are
  where your hands expect them when you type a WiFi password or sign in to your
  account. Every layout has the same letters and the same @ -- only their
  positions change, so nothing you need to type is ever missing. Thanks to
  mickadu57, whose pull request proposed the French layout.

## Changed

- **The scale starts in about 3 seconds instead of about 20.** The TigerTag logo
  still shows for two seconds, but the scale gets everything ready behind it,
  and you can weigh straight away. Connecting to the WiFi now happens in the
  background instead of making you wait in front of a progress bar.
- **Choosing a WiFi network no longer restarts the scale.** After you pick a
  network in Settings, you are taken back to Settings with the new network in
  place. You stay signed in to your account.

## Fixed

- **A scale that could not find its WiFi at start-up stayed offline.** If the
  scale was switched on before its router, or the router restarted, the scale
  stayed disconnected until you touched it. It now tries again on its own every
  30 seconds, and reconnects as soon as the network is back.

## Known issues

- On one bench unit, the right-hand reader reads 7-byte tags (NTAG / TigerTag)
  normally but cannot read 4-byte MIFARE Classic cards. The fault follows the
  reader, not the card, and a second unit does not show it. This looks like a
  hardware fault on that module rather than a firmware problem, and it is not
  believed to affect TigerTag spools, which use 7-byte tags.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update**, or over USB with
`bash scripts/flash.sh --fs`. Both keep your WiFi credentials, TigerTag session
and calibration.
