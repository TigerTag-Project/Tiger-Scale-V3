# v3.2.0

The biggest release since the V3 exists. Account linking now works the first
time — iPhone included — and shows your name seconds after you approve. The
scale speaks nine languages and keeps yours in sync with TigerTag Studio, both
ways. New owners get walked through language, WiFi, account and calibration by
the scale itself. And a redesigned calibration wizard makes getting accurate
weighings a guided two-minute job instead of a leap of faith.

## Added

- **First-boot setup, iPhone-style.** A factory-fresh scale (web installer or
  factory reset) starts by asking your language, then offers WiFi setup and
  account linking — each step skippable, everything still reachable later in
  Settings, and nothing reboots along the way: WiFi connects live and the
  scale carries straight on.
- **Factory reset, from the scale.** Settings > Factory reset erases
  everything — WiFi, account, language, calibration — behind a confirmation
  that says exactly what will be lost, then restarts into the first-boot
  setup. Handing the scale over or starting fresh no longer needs a computer.
- **Your account's language, everywhere.** The scale adopts the language
  saved on your TigerTag account — the same one Studio uses — when you sign
  in, and keeps checking; change the language on the scale and your account
  follows, so Studio picks it up too. Change it in Studio and the scale
  switches, even re-translating the Settings page while you are looking at it.
- **European Portuguese.** The language list is now exactly Studio's, nine
  languages in the same order: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano,
  中文, Português (Brasil), Português (Portugal), Polski. The Brazilian and
  European variants are properly distinct — senha and tela on one side,
  palavra-passe and ecrã on the other.
- **A first-calibration notification.** Until the very first calibration is
  completed, a panel slides in from the right over the home screen — orange
  warning, "Calibrate your TigerScale", calibrate now or later. It returns at
  every boot and every few minutes, because weighings are simply not reliable
  before that first calibration. Once done, it never appears again.

## Changed

- **The calibration wizard was rebuilt end to end.** Three guided steps with
  progress dots in the header, and the header goes back one step instead of
  throwing the whole wizard away. Step 1: empty the platform and press TARE —
  the scale checks the zero actually holds, then moves on by itself. Step 2:
  pick the reference — enter your own weight first (a proper keypad: starts
  empty, whole grams, 150 g to 4500 g, a full-height OK that only turns blue
  when the value is valid) or tap a preset spool. Step 3: place the weight
  and press Calibrate — the button arms only once the reading is steady, so a
  still-swinging spool can never poison the factor. Finishing saves by itself,
  shows the success screen and returns home. The Settings row is now called
  what it is: the Calibration Wizard.
- **Account linking feels finished.** Approving on your phone lands on a real
  success screen — green check, then your name and e-mail as they arrive —
  instead of a bare label next to an empty square. The Settings row shows
  your display name the moment it is known, and the live view now stays
  connected through the entire pairing, so you can follow the QR screen from
  a browser.
- **The home screen tells the truth.** No account linked? A red "No account"
  badge says so — instead of a green "Ready" that could not sync — and
  nothing is sent to the cloud until an account is linked. While a saved
  account signs back in at boot, the badge says "Connecting" in blue.
- **The spool card reads better.** The material name has its own full-width
  line under the brand — "PLA High Speed" arrives whole, not amputated — and
  the weight card carries the same border as its neighbours.

## Fixed

- **QR pairing no longer needs a reboot to show who you are.** After a fresh
  pairing, name and e-mail used to stay at "--" until the next restart; they
  now appear within seconds of approving on the phone.
- **Google sign-in works on iPhone.** The approval page died silently on iOS
  Safari; it now completes the sign-in like everywhere else.
- **Impossible weighings are refused.** A gross weight clearly below the
  spool's own container weight — wrong spool on the platform, drifted tare —
  cancels the send with a red "Weighing error" badge instead of writing a
  negative amount into your inventory.
- **The screen no longer plays dead after Settings.** Touching the screen now
  reliably postpones sleep; coming back from a long stay in the menus used to
  black out the display instantly.
- **An empty platform reads a clean 0** in the calibration wizard — no more
  flicker between "0 g" and "-0 g" — and long confirmation texts fit the
  screen instead of arriving clipped.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- **Over USB** with `bash scripts/flash.sh --fs` — same guarantee.
- **From the browser** via the
  [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/) —
  always installs this latest version, but as a **fresh install**: it resets
  the scale to factory state, and the new first-boot setup then walks you
  back through language, WiFi, account and calibration.
