# v3.2.0

Linking your TigerTag account now works reliably the first time — name and
e-mail appear seconds after you approve on your phone — and the scale speaks
your account's language, keeps it in sync with TigerTag Studio both ways, and
walks new owners through setup and calibration by itself.

## Added

- First-boot setup: a factory-fresh scale (web installer or factory reset)
  asks for your language, offers WiFi setup and account linking — each step
  skippable, nothing reboots along the way.
- Factory reset, from the scale: Settings > Factory reset erases everything
  (WiFi, account, language, calibration) behind a confirmation and restarts
  into the first-boot setup.
- Your account's language follows you: the scale adopts the language saved on
  your TigerTag account (the one Studio uses) when you sign in and on every
  check-in, and changing the language on the scale updates the account — both
  stay aligned with TigerTag Studio.
- European Portuguese. The language list is now exactly Studio's: English,
  Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, 中文, Português (Brasil), Português
  (Portugal), Polski.
- Until the first calibration is done, a notification panel slides in over
  the home screen — calibrate now or later; it returns every few minutes and
  at every boot until a calibration completes, because weighings are not
  reliable before one.

## Changed

- The calibration wizard is three guided steps: empty the platform and TARE
  (the scale checks the zero holds, then moves on by itself), pick the
  reference weight (enter your own first — 150 g to 4500 g — or a preset
  spool), place it and press Calibrate — the button arms only once the
  weight is steady. Finishing saves and returns home.
- Account linking shows a proper success screen (green check, your name and
  e-mail as they arrive) and the live view stays connected through the whole
  pairing, so you can follow it remotely.
- The home screen tells the truth when no account is linked: a red
  "No account" badge instead of a green "Ready" that could not sync — and
  nothing is sent to the cloud until an account is linked.
- The material name has its own full-width line under the brand, so "PLA High
  Speed" no longer arrives truncated; the weight card carries the same border
  as its neighbours.

## Fixed

- QR account pairing: name and e-mail no longer stay at "--" until a reboot
  after a fresh pairing, and Google sign-in works on iPhone.
- A gross weight below the spool's own container weight (wrong spool, drifted
  tare) cancels the send with a red "Weighing error" badge instead of writing
  a negative amount into your inventory.
- Touching the screen reliably postpones screen sleep — coming back from
  Settings no longer blacks out the display instantly.
- Settings shows your display name as soon as it arrives after pairing,
  instead of freezing on "OK".

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update**, or over USB with
`bash scripts/flash.sh --fs`. Both keep your WiFi credentials, TigerTag session
and calibration.
