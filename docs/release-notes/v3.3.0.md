# v3.3.0

A scale with no linked account now offers the connection itself: a panel
slides in over the home screen — "Connect your account", one blue Sign in
button — instead of waiting for you to find the option in Settings.

## Added

- **The scale invites the connection.** When no account is linked and WiFi
  is up, a side panel slides in over the home screen shortly after boot:
  "Connect your account", with a blue Sign in button that opens the usual
  sign-in options (e-mail, or the QR code for Google). "Later" folds it away
  — it only comes back every half hour, and never during a weighing.
  Linking stays optional: the scale keeps working offline exactly as before.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- **Over USB** with `bash scripts/flash.sh --fs` — same guarantee.
- **From the browser** via the
  [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/) —
  always installs this latest version, but as a **fresh install**: it resets
  the scale to factory state and the first-boot setup walks you back through
  language, WiFi, account and calibration.
