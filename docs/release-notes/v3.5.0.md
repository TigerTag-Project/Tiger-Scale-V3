# v3.5.0

The battery finally behaves like the one in your pocket: the charge level is
written inside the icon, the bolt tracks whether current is actually flowing,
and plugging a cable in shows up straight away instead of up to half a minute
later. Your account sees all of it too. And a scale that had quietly settled on
a distant access point now picks the nearest one.

## Added

- **The charge level is written inside the battery icon.** A single fill whose
  width is the level, green while charging, red at or below 20%, and the
  lightning bolt beside the outline. The bolt now follows the charger itself —
  a full battery on a live cable stops showing it, the way a phone does, instead
  of claiming to charge forever.
- **Your account knows about the battery.** The scale reports whether a battery
  is fitted, its level, whether it is charging and whether it is running on USB
  or on the cell. A scale with no battery reports nothing rather than reporting
  zero, so an app can tell "no battery" from "flat".
- **A scale in standby says so.** With the backlight off it keeps checking in,
  five minutes apart instead of thirty seconds, and announces the moment it goes
  into or comes out of standby. Apps can show "in standby" instead of guessing
  from a timestamp that stopped moving.

## Changed

- **The WiFi signal is drawn the same way everywhere.** The weigh screen and the
  network picker share one icon and one scale, over four levels. The picker
  follows the macOS layout — the signal ahead of the network name, the connected
  one in a blue bubble — and its five vertical bars are gone.
- **Plugging and unplugging register in a quarter of a second**, where it used
  to take up to thirty. The screen only repaints when something actually
  changed, so the faster reading costs nothing.

## Fixed

- **Scales connect to the nearest access point.** On a network with several
  access points sharing one name — a mesh, or a router broadcasting several
  networks — a scale used to settle on whichever radio answered first and stay
  there for good, even after a reboot. The bench unit was sitting 43 dB below
  what a router one metre away could give it. Scales now scan properly and pick
  the strongest. **The first start after this update reconnects from scratch**;
  your WiFi credentials are kept.
- **A scale with its screen off no longer disappears from your account.** It was
  never asleep — the screen going dark simply stopped it reporting in.
- **The battery icon renders correctly.** Its terminal nub was being clipped
  away, leaving a plain rectangle, and the signal icon lost the top of its wave.
- **Scrollbars no longer sit on top of list content** on any settings screen.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- Or from the [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/),
  which always serves the current release.
