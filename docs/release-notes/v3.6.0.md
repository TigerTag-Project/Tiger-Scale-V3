# v3.6.0

Fixes a battery reading that could show you the wrong number, and adds the
Restart button that a scale with a battery in it needs.

## Fixed

- **The battery level could show only half its digits.** Between roughly 50%
  and 80% the second figure vanished — a scale at 55% displayed "5". The number
  was there, drawn in a dark colour that happened to fall on the dark part of
  the icon. The percentage is now always readable, whatever the level.

## Added

- **Settings has a Restart button**, just above Factory reset. With a battery
  fitted there was no way to restart the scale by hand: unplugging the USB cable
  no longer switches it off, it simply runs on the battery. Restart asks for
  confirmation and keeps everything — WiFi, account, calibration.

## Changed

- **On battery, the level bar is blue**; it stays green while charging and red
  when low. The old white bar is what made the digit disappear, so the colour
  had to move for the reading to be trustworthy.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- Or from the [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/),
  which always serves the current release.
