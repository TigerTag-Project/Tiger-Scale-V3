# v3.7.1

The scale tells your apps what it is doing the moment it happens, instead of
roughly half the time. Plug a cable in, unplug it, let the screen go dark — each
of those now reaches your account within a second or two.

## Fixed

- **State changes were being dropped.** When the screen went dark, when a cable
  went in or out, the scale was supposed to report it straight away. It only did
  so if at least thirty seconds had passed since its last report — otherwise the
  news was thrown away rather than sent late. Roughly one change in two announced
  nothing, which is why an app could show a scale as disconnected while it was
  sitting there awake, or take a minute to notice the cable was out.
- **The charging bolt flickered on a nearly full battery.** Close to full, the
  charger really does stop and restart while it tops the cell off, and the scale
  was showing every one of those — seventeen changes in under a minute at 94%.
  The bolt now stays lit through the top-off and goes out when the charge is
  genuinely over.
- **Unplugging shows instantly.** A first attempt at the fix above also smoothed
  the cable being pulled, which made unplugging take ten seconds to appear.
- **The battery icon no longer blinks off.** An occasional failed reading of the
  power chip was being reported as "no battery fitted".
- **The charge level is up to date when you plug in**, rather than showing a
  figure from up to five minutes earlier next to a live charging status.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- Or from the [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/),
  which always serves the current release.
