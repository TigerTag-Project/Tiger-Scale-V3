# v3.7.2

A maintenance release. Nothing changes on the scale itself — this one is for
whoever has to work out why a scale is behaving oddly without being able to plug
a cable into it.

## Added

- **Cloud activity is readable over the network.** The scale kept its record of
  talking to your account on the USB port only, so it vanished the moment the
  cable came out — exactly when a scale that has stopped appearing in an app
  needs looking at. Failed syncs and the moments that matter — going to sleep,
  waking up, the cable going in or out — are now in the log the scale serves on
  your network, alongside the screen state and whether it is running on USB or on
  the battery.

  Routine syncs stay out of it deliberately: the log keeps about eighty lines,
  and one entry every thirty seconds would bury the weighings and tag reads it is
  there for within the hour.

## Known issues

- On-tag metadata reads still fall back to the cached values; a spool never
  seen before may show no brand or material.

## Updating

- **Over the air** from **Settings > Update** — keeps your WiFi credentials,
  TigerTag session and calibration.
- Or from the [web installer](https://tigertag-project.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/),
  which always serves the current release.
